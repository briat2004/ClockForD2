# Privacy Policy — Clock for Diablo II

_Last updated: 2026-10-18_

Clock for Diablo II ("the app") is an unofficial companion app that shows community-reported Diablo Clone progress and Terror Zone rotations for Diablo II: Resurrected. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

## What the app collects

The app does not collect any personal information. It has no accounts and no advertising.

**Anonymous usage analytics.** The app uses Google Firebase Analytics (the variant without advertising identifiers) to understand which features are used. Firebase records which buttons are tapped (for example "opened settings", "picked a server", "toggled a reminder"), together with app version, device model, OS version, language and country. This data is aggregated, not linked to your identity, and not used for advertising or tracking across apps. It is processed by Google under the [Firebase privacy terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Network requests

To show live data, the app sends requests to **d2runewizard.com** (a third-party community site). These requests contain only:

- the data being requested (server progress, Terror Zone, planned walks),
- a fixed developer contact address and app identifier required by that site's fair-use policy.

They do not include any information about you or your device beyond what any standard HTTPS request carries (such as your IP address, which d2runewizard.com may log under its own privacy policy).

## Data stored on your device

Your preferences (tracked server, favorite Terror Zones, alert switches) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

# 隱私權政策 — Clock for Diablo II

_最後更新：2026-10-18_

Clock for Diablo II（以下稱「本 App」）是非官方的輔助工具，顯示《暗黑破壞神 II：獄火重生》玩家回報的地表暗黑進度與恐怖地帶輪換，與 Blizzard Entertainment 無關。

## 本 App 收集什麼

本 App 不收集任何個人資訊；沒有帳號、沒有廣告。

**匿名使用分析。** 本 App 使用 Google Firebase Analytics（不含廣告識別碼的版本）了解哪些功能被使用：記錄按了哪些按鈕（例如「開啟設定」、「選擇伺服器」、「切換提醒開關」），以及 App 版本、裝置型號、系統版本、語言與國家。這些資料是彙整統計、不會與你的身分連結、不用於廣告或跨 App 追蹤，由 Google 依 [Firebase 隱私條款](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) 處理。

## 網路請求

為了顯示即時資料，本 App 會向第三方社群網站 **d2runewizard.com** 發出請求，內容只有：

- 要查詢的資料（伺服器進度、恐怖地帶、預告開地表），
- 該網站公平使用政策要求的固定開發者聯絡方式與 App 識別。

不包含任何關於你或你裝置的資訊（一般 HTTPS 請求本身會帶的 IP 位址除外，d2runewizard.com 可能依其隱私權政策記錄）。

## 儲存在你裝置上的資料

你的偏好設定（追蹤伺服器、喜愛的恐怖地帶、提醒開關）只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 即一併刪除。

## 通知

若你允許通知，本 App 會在你的裝置上排程本機通知；通知內容不會離開你的裝置。

## 兒童

本 App 不會收集任何人的資訊，包括兒童。
